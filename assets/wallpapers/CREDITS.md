# Wallpaper credits

Backgrounds behind the timer, picked with the shuffle button or the W key. Two groups:

- **25 animated character loops**: cozy, lofi and cute scenes.
- **20 still wallpapers**: character art at 1920x1080.

Loops are 1280x720, 14 seconds, muted, made seamless with a one second crossfade back into the start, served as AV1 WebM with an H.264 MP4 fallback and a JPG poster. Everything comes from MoeWalls (moewalls.com) and is fan-made; rights stay with the original artists and studios. If you own one and want it removed, open an issue.
